FAQ

Common questions

Am I an employee of ZENI or an independent contractor? +
You are an independent contractor on the ZENI platform. You set your own hours, choose which bookings to accept, and are free to work on other platforms simultaneously. ZENI does not control your schedule or require minimum hours. Your contractor agreement will clearly define this relationship.
How quickly does payout happen? +
Payout is released the moment the client confirms the detail is complete. If you have instant payout enabled (available via Stripe), funds arrive in your bank account within minutes. Standard payout is next business day. There are no holding periods or minimum balances.
What is the platform fee? +
ZENI charges an 11% platform fee on the total booking value. This covers payment processing, background check monitoring, liability insurance facilitation, app infrastructure, and customer support. The rates shown on this page are your earnings after the fee has been deducted — no surprises.
What if a client behaves inappropriately? +
You have the right to end any detail at any time if you feel unsafe or if the client's behavior violates ZENI's terms. ZENI's ops team is available 24/7 via the professional app. All incidents are logged in a tamper-evident system. Clients who violate our code of conduct are permanently removed from the platform. Your safety is not negotiable.
Do I need to maintain my own insurance? +
ZENI facilitates $1M per-detail liability coverage for bookings made through the platform. This does not replace your personal professional liability insurance if you carry it — both can apply. Specific coverage terms are detailed in the professional contractor agreement. We recommend consulting a licensed insurance professional regarding your individual coverage needs.
Can I work in multiple cities? +
Yes, with some important compliance requirements. Each city and state has its own security licensing laws. Before accepting bookings in a new jurisdiction, ZENI will verify that your credentials are valid there. You set your operating radius in the app and can update it any time.
